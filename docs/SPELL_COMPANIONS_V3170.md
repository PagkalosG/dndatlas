# v3.17.0 — Spell and feature companions

Cumulative release from main v3.12.0 including v3.13–v3.16. Upgrade the reviewed encounter Worker before frontend deployment. No new table or network request.

Character and Combat pages expose Spell & feature companions. The DM Party and Battle cards show the same character-owned linkedCompanions record. Availability: owned spellbook entries for Find Familiar, Find Steed, Summon Beast and Summon Fey; Druid 2 Wild Companion; Paladin 5 Faithful Steed; Warlock with the recorded Pact of the Chain feature. Investment of the Chain Master requires Warlock 5 and both recorded invocations. No automatic grant merely for being a Wizard or Warlock. Record the selected invocation in Features. Familiar forms use the SRD 5.2.1 creature catalog; Slaad Tadpole uses verified HP/AC plus a link to the official reference, not a full copied stat block.

Casting from the existing spell dialog routes through the linked companion form. Confirm completed casting and components, select a legal source and available slot/ritual/feature/resource. No material inventory deduction or in-game time automation. Wild Companion uses a slot or Wild Shape use and is removed on Long Rest; Faithful Steed free use resets on Long Rest. One familiar and one steed per caster, plus at most one supported concentration summon. Recasting replaces the relevant record. Supported summons disappear at zero HP, concentration end/change or rest; the DM/player must manually end a summon when an hour of in-game time expires. Find Familiar dismissal/return and release are recorded manually. Steed and familiar HP persist in the sheet. Steed ability recharge and owner-death disappearance are manual adjudication; no automatic Life Bond. Multi-class spell attack uses the recorded class source or sheet spellcasting ability fallback, not a universal forced Wisdom bonus.

Turn rules are explained on each card: familiar separate initiative, steed shared initiative, summoned beast/fey immediately after caster. This update does not add separate linked initiative rows to the encounter engine or automatically apply turn order. DM HP editing is through the shared sheet card, not the combat log target selector. No extra generic copies are created. Existing Save/character revision and authorization checks apply; dedicated validation of the new metadata at the server remains outstanding. Generic monster source text remains English. Other summons and Steel Defender not implemented; revised Artificer is in Eberron: Forge of the Artificer (2025), and requires a compatible class implementation rather than silently reusing the 2014 version.
