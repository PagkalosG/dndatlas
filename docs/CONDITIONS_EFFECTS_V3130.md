# v3.13.0 — Conditions & Effects (D&D 2024)

DM / Creator: in Multiview → Battle View, open Conditions & Effects during an active or paused encounter. Choose a target (player, enemy, ally), a 2024 condition, a named temporary effect or a concentration spell. Set a manual ending, a round plus start/end-of-turn expiration reminder, or a repeatable saving throw with ability and DC. The reminders are not automatic rules adjudication. At an end-of-turn boundary, the reminder appears after advancing past that combatant. Previous Turn does not undo already confirmed resolutions. For an effect requiring a different duration, enter the correct round and timing from its rules.

A caster can have only one concentration effect; starting another or recording an incapacitating encounter condition ends it and removes linked effects/checks. Applied damage from the DM HP or combat log tools queues a separate Constitution save for each damage instance using the final damage entered, including damage absorbed by temporary HP. DC = min(30, max(10, floor(damage/2))). For damage only recorded elsewhere, use Damage check manually. Resolve each pending check using physical dice; a failed check removes the concentration and its linked effects. Death or incapacitation not entered in this panel must be adjudicated manually. This encounter-only tracker does not replace character-sheet conditions, exhaustion calculations or spell/resource choices, nor enforce movement or attack modifiers. End Encounter clears encounter effects, while existing activity logs remain archived.

Changes stay in the local encounter draft until the main Save. Server validates bounded effects, participant references, concentration links, DCs and roles; public player projections do not include effects or enemy details. No new D1 tables, permissions, bindings or automated dice. Old snapshots remain readable.

Deploy the upgraded production Worker encounter extension **before** deploying the frontend. Use the existing reviewed patch-v3100-encounter.py with the new encounter-extension.js; exact v3.12.0 extension retained for safe replacement. Verify staging D1 integration before rollout. No production D1 migration required.
